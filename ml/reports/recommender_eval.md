# Recommender evaluation

> **Synthetic data.** The customers and orders in this evaluation were generated by
> `bin/build_eval_db.php`, which gives each customer favourite categories and a dosha and
> makes them buy accordingly. The numbers show that the code can recover those built-in
> patterns. They are **not** evidence of how well it works for real customers.

- Date: 2026-11-27
- Database: `osuthuru_eval`
- Products: 57, orders: 708, customers tested: 199
- Method: leave-one-out. One product from each customer's latest order is hidden; each model
  suggests 5 products the customer has not bought.

| Model | precision@5 | recall@5 | hits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Popularity baseline | 0.027 | 0.136 | 27 |
| Hybrid | 0.083 | 0.417 | 83 |

With one hidden product per customer, the best possible precision@5 is 0.200.
